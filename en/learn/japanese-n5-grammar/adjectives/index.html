Adjectives and comparison – Japanese N5 grammar

い and な adjectives, 〜くないです, 〜より, いちばん – seven points on adjective inflection and on comparing things.

  • い形容詞 · an adjective ending in い
  • な形容詞 · an adjective that requires な
  • 〜くないです · is not so
  • 〜かったです · was so
  • 〜くなる · becomes so
  • 〜より · than…, more than…
  • いちばん · the most…, the best of…

い形容詞 · an adjective ending in い

an inflecting adjective, ending in い

It inflects on its own, without help from です — and です is added purely for politeness and carries no tense. Before a noun it stands with no extra ending at all.

い-adjective + noun; as a predicate: adjective + です

Examples

  • この本ほんはおもしろいです。This book is interesting.です does not inflect the adjective, it only raises the register.
  • 新あたらしいかばんを買かいました。I bought a new bag.Before a noun the adjective does not change its shape.

な形容詞 · an adjective that requires な

an adjective that adds な before a noun

It behaves more like a noun than like an adjective. Before the word it modifies it has to insert な, and as a predicate it inflects through です, exactly as a noun does.

adjective + な + noun; as a predicate: adjective + です

Examples

  • ここは静しずかな町まちです。This is a quiet town.Without な the sentence would be ungrammatical.
  • 彼女かのじょは親切しんせつな人ひとです。She is a kind person.The same pattern: modifier + な + the noun modified.

〜くないです · is not so

negating a quality described by an i-adjective

The negation inflects the adjective itself, not です. The final い drops out and くない comes in — which is why いいです does not give いくないです but よくないです.

adjective without い + くないです

Examples

  • この店みせは高たかくないです。This shop is not expensive.高い → 高くない. です stays unchanged.
  • 今日きょうはあまり寒さむくないです。It is not particularly cold today.あまり forms a fixed pattern with negation — see あまり〜ない in group 7.

〜かったです · was so

a quality placed in the past

The past tense is likewise written into the adjective itself. A common mistake is おもしろいでした — です does not turn into でした when an い-adjective is inflected.

adjective without い + かったです

Examples

  • 昨日きのうの試験しけんはむずかしかったです。Yesterday’s exam was difficult.むずかしい → むずかしかった, not むずかしいでした.
  • 旅行りょこうはとても楽たのしかったです。The trip was very enjoyable.The same inflection pattern.

〜くなる · becomes so

a shift into a new state, with no one named as its cause

Describes a passage from one state into another. It does not settle whether the change happened by itself or whether someone or something brought it about — it simply does not say who or what did it, and a cause may well exist. With な-adjectives and with nouns, に comes in instead of く.

い-adjective without い + くなる; な-adjective + になる

Examples

  • 春はるになって、暖あたたかくなりました。Spring came and it turned warm.A change of season is hard to attribute to anyone — but the form くなる itself does not settle that.
  • 日本語にほんごが少すこしずつ易やさしくなりました。Japanese has gradually become easier.少しずつ stresses that the passage was gradual.

〜より · than…, more than…

marking which of two things comes out higher

Sets exactly two things side by side. The order matters: より stands next to the one that loses the comparison, not the winner — the opposite of English “than”, which follows the adjective.

A は B より + adjective

Examples

  • 電車でんしゃはバスより速はやいです。The train is faster than the bus.より stands next to the bus, that is, next to the slower one.
  • 今日きょうは昨日きのうより暑あついです。It is hotter today than yesterday.Two moments in time can be compared as well.

いちばん · the most…, the best of…

the superlative within a stated set

Japanese does not grade adjectives with an ending — it puts a separate word in front of them. The set the comparison is made within is marked by で or の中で.

set + で / の中で + いちばん + adjective

Examples

  • クラスでいちばん背せが高たかいです。He is the tallest in the class.The class is the set the comparison is drawn within.
  • 四季しきの中なかで秋あきがいちばん好すきです。Of all the seasons I like autumn best.With a countable set の中で is the more natural choice.
