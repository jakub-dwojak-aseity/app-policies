What this app is

Bunmyaku is a tool for studying Japanese vocabulary on your own, at JLPT levels N5 through N1. The learning — reviews, every question type, the study plan and your progress — runs entirely on your device and without an internet connection. The app can also read Japanese text from a photo so you can collect vocabulary of your own. Version 1.2 adds optional AI features, which use a server and which you start yourself. This is not a taught course, a certification service, or preparation that guarantees passing the JLPT.

The AI features — an auto-renewing subscription

The AI features are separate and optional. There are two: Analyze breaks into words a sentence you read from a photo and confirmed, and Write a sentence with AI composes a short example sentence for a chosen entry in its dictionary form. They are the only part of the app that costs something every time it is used, which is why they are billed as a subscription rather than sold outright.

  • Every month you get a free allowance of calls, at no charge and without a subscription. The app shows how many are left and when the allowance comes back.
  • The subscription is available monthly or yearly and renews automatically until you turn it off. The app shows the price and the length of the period before you buy, in your currency.

  • The AI features need an internet connection and may be temporarily unavailable — during maintenance, for example, or an outage at the model provider. The rest of the app keeps working normally.

Your data

Your progress and the words and sentences you add are saved only on your device. Keeping them safe is up to you — deleting the app deletes that data. Use Export progress in Settings to keep a copy.

The AI features are the exception, and only when you start them: what goes to the server is one sentence you confirmed, or one entry from your vocabulary. Your study history does not, nothing that could identify you does, and a photo never does — text in a photo is read by the phone itself. What is stored, and for how long, is described in the privacy policy.

Accuracy of the content

The material was prepared with due care and is checked automatically, but natural language is more complex than any simplification of it. Meanings and examples are educational and do not constitute a linguistic norm. If you find an error, write in — corrections go into later releases.

This applies to the AI features separately: their answers are generated by a language model and can be wrong. They are a hint, not an exam grade or a teacher's opinion — treat them as a starting point for checking, not as a verdict. That goes especially for words and sentences brought into your study this way: you see them before they are added, and you decide what goes in.
