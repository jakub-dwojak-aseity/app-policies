What this app is

Shindan 診断 is a Japanese level test with a diagnosis. It is not the Japanese Language Proficiency Test, is not affiliated with the Japan Foundation or JEES, and is not endorsed by them. It refers to JLPT levels descriptively, to name the scale its material is aligned with.

What the result means

It is an estimate from one sheet, not a certificate and not a prediction. It is useful for deciding what to study next. It says nothing binding about whether you will pass any exam.

Purchases

The first sheet is free. Further sheets are a one-time purchase, non-transferable, sold through the App Store under Apple's terms.

The debrief

From version 1.2 a finished sheet can get a sentence about what your mistakes have in common. A language model writes it, and it is the only feature that uses the network; you start it yourself, and it comes with the purchase. It is an addition to the result, not the result: your level and your share of mistakes are worked out by the app on your phone, and they are the same with the debrief and without it.

The model can be wrong. A sentence about a pattern is a hint about what to look at next, not a verdict — where it contradicts the numbers on screen, the numbers are right. The feature may also fail to answer for a while; that costs you neither the result nor the sheet.

What it sends, and what it does not, is set out in the privacy policy.
