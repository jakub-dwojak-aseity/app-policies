What it looks like

Description from the store

ころころ or ごろごろ? Learn to tell them apart in a sentence.

ころころ describes something small and light rolling. ごろごろ describes something big and heavy. Compare similar words, read translated examples and learn which clues point to the right choice.

Practice four skills: choosing the word that fits a sentence, meaning, word shape and attachment, and the difference voicing makes. Answer with tiles, without typing. Each answer leads to an explanation and the word's reference card.

Word cards include meanings, examples with furigana and translations, clues and similar words. Pair comparisons explain the nuance and show examples of both words. Your progress and mistakes stay on your device.

Six levels cover the body and feelings, voiced pairs, emotions, word shapes, the world around you, and sentence attachment. The first two levels are completely free. Unlock the remaining levels with one non-renewing purchase.

Words return for spaced review. See which similar words you mix up and in which direction.

In Sentences, paste a line from a manga or scan it from a photo. AI explains the onomatopoeia in that context. You can also describe a sound or feeling and find words that fit. A free query allowance is available; a separate auto-renewing subscription provides a larger allowance. Prices and terms are shown before purchase. The subscription is not required for practice or levels.

Learning works offline. AI requires internet access. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off by default; withdraw your consent in Settings. No account or ads.

Common questions

What does Onomatope teach?

Onomatope teaches Japanese onomatopoeia and mimetic words in sentences: you compare similar words such as ころころ and ごろごろ and learn which clues point to the right one.

What is free and how does paid access work?

The first two of six levels – body and feeling, and voicing pairs – are free in full. The other four are unlocked with one non-renewing purchase.

AI help in the Sentences tab is a separate, optional subscription; buying levels does not include it. You will see the current price in your App Store currency in the app before you confirm the purchase.

How many AI requests do I get?

The Sentences tab explains the mimetics in a pasted line or suggests words for a description. You get 5 free requests per calendar month. The “AI Help” subscription gives up to 30 requests a day and up to 300 in each month of the subscription, counted from the purchase date (a yearly subscription refills that allowance every month).

The subscription is separate from buying the material – the one-time purchase does not include it, and you do not need it for practice or levels. AI needs a connection. You will see the current price in your App Store currency in the app before you confirm the purchase.

Does Onomatope work offline and without an account?

Learning works offline, and your progress and mistakes stay on the phone. Only these need a connection: purchases and restoring them, AI in the Sentences tab and optional anonymous statistics – off by default; withdraw consent in Settings.

The app has no account or sign-in of its own; purchases go through your Apple Account.

What happens when I change phones?

Restore your purchase on the new phone with “Restore purchases” in the app, signed in with the same Apple Account – this covers the AI subscription too.

Your study progress is separate: it stays on the phone, so you move it with the backup file – export it in Settings on the old phone and import it on the new one.

What languages does Onomatope support?

In Polish and in English.

Which devices does Onomatope run on?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. The app is designed for iPhone.
