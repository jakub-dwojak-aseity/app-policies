Shivering, trembling and dizziness – Japanese mimetics

Meaning, explanation and example sentences with translations.

  • くらくら · to feel dizzy
  • ぐらぐら · to wobble
  • びりびり · to tingle sharply
  • ぶるぶる · to tremble in small shivers
  • がくがく · to shake at the joints
  • じんじん · to tingle and throb
  • ぞくぞく · to shiver down the spine
  • ひくひく · to twitch faintly

くらくら · to feel dizzy

The room spins.

Voiceless く is lighter: your head sways, not the furniture. くらくら names the sensation in the head itself — the room turns, everything goes dark; ふらふら names something else: that nothing is settled, neither the step nor the thought. Both can end with you crouching down, so failing to stay on your feet settles nothing here — what settles it is whether the world spins or your head fogs over. ぐるぐる, by contrast, describes a thing that really turns round, not the person looking.

Examples

  • 健康診断けんこうしんだんの採血さいけつのあと、目めの前まえが真まっ暗くらになって天井てんじょうが回まわり、その場ばにしゃがみ込こむほどくらくらした。After the blood draw at my check-up everything went black, the ceiling turned, and my head swam so badly that I crouched down where I stood.
  • サウナに長ながく入はいりすぎて、天井てんじょうが回まわるようにくらくらし、立たっていられずしゃがみ込こんだ。I stayed in the sauna too long; my head is spinning as if the ceiling were turning, and I had to crouch because I could not stay on my feet.

ぐらぐら · to wobble

An unsteady thing: a table, a tooth, a shelf.

Voiced ぐ is heavier and bigger — an object wobbles, not a feeling. What separates the neighbours is what has happened to the fixing: ぐらぐら wobbles because its mounting has given way and the thing is about to go over; がたがた knocks and rattles because there is play between the parts; ゆらゆら sways calmly, with nothing at stake.

Examples

  • ねじが緩ゆるんだ眼鏡めがねのつるが、音おともなく片側かたがわへ傾かたむいて揺ゆれ、ぐらぐらして掛かけていられない。A screw in my glasses has worked loose: the temple tips silently to one side and wobbles too much to wear them.
  • 抜ぬけかけの乳歯にゅうしが、指ゆびで押おすと片側かたがわへ傾かたむき、落おち着つかないほどぐらぐらと動うごく。The loose milk tooth already gives: press it with a finger and it tips to one side, wobbling too much to ignore.

びりびり · to tingle sharply

Electricity through the body; also: tearing.

Voiced び is strong and sudden — it does not burn, it jolts. What separates it from じんじん is time: びりびり passes through like a discharge and is gone, while じんじん stays long after the cause has stopped.

Examples

  • 肘ひじをぶつけて、電気でんきが走はしったように腕うでがびりびりした。I banged my elbow and a jolt like electricity shot through my arm.
  • ドアノブに触ふれた瞬間しゅんかん、指先ゆびさきにびりびりと静電気せいでんきが走はしった。The moment I touched the doorknob, static electricity shot through my fingertips.

ぶるぶる · to tremble in small shivers

Shaking from cold or from fear.

The 濁点 in ぶ makes the trembling involuntary — from cold or from fear, and impossible to stop. Plain ふるふる is light and faint: it is how you shake your head to say no (ふるふると首を振る), not how you shiver in the cold. The four neighbours each leave along a different axis: がくがく shakes coarsely, at the whole joint, until the knees give, while ぶるぶる trembles finely just under the skin; ぞくぞく does not tremble at all, it runs down the back as a chill; びくびく stays inside, being a person's fear rather than a movement of the body; ぷるぷる quivers softly and pleasantly, and is said of a thing, not of someone cold.

Examples

  • 上着うわぎを忘わすれ、歯はの根ねが合あわないほどの寒さむさで、はたから見みても分わかるくらい体からだが小刻こきざみにぶるぶると震ふるえる。I forgot my jacket and the cold sets my teeth chattering; my whole body shivers in small tremors plain enough for anyone to see.
  • 恐怖きょうふで歯はの根ねが合あわず、震ふるえが指先ゆびさきまで及およんで、はたから見みても分わかるくらい小刻こきざみにぶるぶるしている。Fear sets my teeth chattering; the tremor reaches my fingertips and anyone can see it.

がくがく · to shake at the joints

Knees that shake and give way.

The amplitude sits in が, not in the 濁点 as such — ぶるぶる carries one too and still shakes finely. Open が lets the whole joint go; closed ぶ keeps the shaking at the skin. がたがた shakes just as coarsely, but a thing — a window, a cart, a rickety table — and it rattles while it does; がくがく is about the joints of a living body.

Examples

  • 長ながい下くだり坂ざかを歩あるいたあと、膝ひざが笑わらうほど大おおきくがくがくと震ふるえた。After the long walk down the slope my knees shook so hard they almost gave way.
  • 面接めんせつの順番じゅんばんを待まつうちに、膝ひざが言いうことを聞きかず、大おおきくがくがくしてきた。As I waited my turn for the interview, my knees stopped obeying me and my legs began to shake.

じんじん · to tingle and throb

Numbness that pulses and burns.

The 濁点 in じ makes the sensation deep and diffuse. Plain しんしん drifted off to weather and silence. What separates it from びりびり is time: びりびり jolts once and is gone, while じんじん stays long after the cause. What separates it from ずきずき is the kind of sensation: ずきずき is pain itself, じんじん is numbness that happens to pulse.

Examples

  • 正座せいざを崩くずしたあと、痛いたいほどのしびれで足あしが脈打みゃくうつようにじんじんと痛いたむ。When I came out of the formal kneeling position my legs went numb and now they throb painfully.
  • 氷水こおりみずから出だした指先ゆびさきが、痛いたいほどのしびれで脈打みゃくうつようにじんじんと熱ねつを持もった。My fingertips, taken out of the ice water, went numb to the point of pain and began to throb with heat.

ぞくぞく · to shiver down the spine

A chill down the back: fever or horror.

There is no plain そくそく in this sense, so the 濁点 settles nothing here — and neither does the shiver itself: ぞくぞく can be pleasant (楽しみでぞくぞくする). This card teaches the other one, from cold and from horror. A chill runs, it does not shake: ぶるぶる is a visible trembling of the body, while ぞくぞく goes down the back and need not show on you at all. ぞっと passes through once and is over at once; ぞくぞく keeps coming back in waves.

Examples

  • 風邪かぜの引ひき始はじめか、さっきから何度なんども寒気さむけが背筋せすじを走はしってぞくぞくする。I think I am coming down with a cold; for a while now chills have been running down my spine again and again.
  • 怪談かいだんを聞きいてから、思おもい出だすたびに、うなじの毛けが逆立さかだつような寒気さむけがぞくぞくと背筋せすじを走はしる。Ever since I heard that ghost story, every time it comes back to me a chill runs down my spine and makes the hair on the back of my neck stand up.

ひくひく · to twitch faintly

An eyelid or the corner of a mouth twitching on its own.

Voiceless ひ keeps the movement microscopic and neutral. The 濁点 form びくびく would turn a muscle twitch into a person's fear.

Examples

  • 寝不足ねぶそくのせいか、まぶたが同おなじリズムで小ちいさく震ふるえてひくひくする。From lack of sleep, perhaps, my eyelid keeps twitching faintly in the same rhythm.
  • 笑わらいをこらえて、口くちの端はしが同おなじリズムで小ちいさくひくひくと震ふるえた。As I held back a laugh, the corner of my mouth twitched faintly in the same rhythm.
