Kuzushi — Casual Japanese

iPhone · version 1.0.0 · English and Polish

Textbooks teach 〜ている and 〜てしまう. People say 〜てる and 〜ちゃった. Kuzushi trains the gap between the two — in both directions.

What it does

You already learned the full forms. The problem is hearing them inside what people actually say. Kuzushi drills the transformation itself: recognizing a shortened form and producing one, as two separate skills, across five practical levels of casual speech — not exam levels, because 〜ちゃう and 〜なきゃ belong to no exam and turn up in the first minute of a conversation.

What it does not do

No account, no network, no analytics, no advertising, no AI. Everything ships inside the app and stays on your device. Levels 1 and 2 are free in full; one non-consumable purchase opens the rest, with no subscription.

The other apps

Kuzushi is the sixth of six Japanese apps by the same author: Kaname (grammar), Katsuyokei (conjugation), Joshi (particles), Kazoekata (counters) and Bunmyaku (reading). Their documents are in the same place. Kaname teaches what 〜てしまう means; Kuzushi teaches you to hear it inside 食べちゃった. Different skills, learned separately.
