Privacy Policy

Kuzushi · version 1.0.0 · last updated 27 August 2026

Short version: Kuzushi collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server. The app makes no network request of any kind except to the App Store, for the purchase itself.

Your account

There isn't one. Kuzushi does not ask you to register, sign in or provide an email address, and it has no way to identify you.

Where your learning data goes

Nowhere. Your mastery of each pattern, your answer history and your session history are stored on your device and never leave it. If you export a backup, the resulting file is yours — it is written wherever you choose to save it, and the app does not upload it.

Data shared with the developer's other apps

If you also have Kaname, Katsuyokei, Joshi, Kazoekata or Bunmyaku installed, those apps and Kuzushi share a small local file — a few hundred bytes with one progress percentage, a streak count, a session count and one sentence about your weakest spot. This file lives inside a shared container on your device. It is not sent anywhere, it is not readable by other developers' apps, and it exists only so that the "Your Japanese" section can show all of your Japanese study in one place.

Reminders

The evening reminder is optional and entirely local. If you turn it on, iOS schedules the notification on the device; nothing is sent anywhere and no server is involved. If you decline the notification permission, the rest of the app works exactly as before.

Tracking and advertising

None. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no tracking identifier of any kind in the app.

Purchases

The one-off Premium purchase is handled entirely by Apple. Kuzushi never sees your payment details; it only asks StoreKit whether a purchase exists.

Reporting a problem

If you use "Report a problem" in Settings, your mail app opens with a message you can read and edit before sending. It includes the app version, the build number, the iOS version and the device model series — never a device identifier. You can delete those lines before sending.

Children

Kuzushi is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated date at the top changes with it.
