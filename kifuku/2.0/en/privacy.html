Privacy Policy

Kifuku 起伏 · version 2.0 · last updated: 15 November 2026

Kifuku 起伏 teaches Japanese pitch accent — where the tone drops inside a word. All learning happens on your device and works without an internet connection. From version 2.0 you can also explicitly send a problem report from an answer card, and send an idea or a bug description through a form in Settings. You give an email address there only if you want a reply. This policy covers Kifuku 2.0; versions 1.x use the earlier policy.

In short: no account and no advertising, and learning works offline. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off by default. Your answers and progress stay on your device. Recordings are embedded in the app, not streamed. An answer-card report and a feedback-form message leave your device only when you press “Send”.

Your account

There isn't one. Kifuku never asks you to register or sign in, and has no way of recognising you. The only exception is an email address you may type into the feedback form yourself if you want a reply — see Feedback form.

Where your learning data goes

Your mastery of each item, your answer history and your review schedule stay on your device and never leave it. If you agree to usage statistics (below), only counts in ranges are sent — never the answers themselves. The exception is a copy of one question and your answer that you send yourself in an answer-card report.

Network connections

Learning, recordings and progress need no internet connection. The app connects to our server api.jd-japanese.pl in three situations only: usage statistics — after your consent; an answer-card report — only after you press “Send”; the feedback form — only after you press “Send”. Purchases and restoring purchases are handled by the App Store.

Optional usage statistics

We ask once during first use or after an update. No events are collected or sent before consent. You can decline or withdraw consent in Settings → Your data, using Share anonymous usage statistics. Turning it off stops transmission and deletes pending events. Learning works the same after declining and without internet.

After consent, the app sends events to https://api.jd-japanese.pl/v1/events about first consent, closing the introduction, the first completed session, a completed session on a later study day, the first completed review, active study days, session endings, viewing the full-version offer and a completed purchase. Every event carries the event name and schema version, the app name, app version and feature variant, the version and variant at first consent, the first-use week, the study-day number, the interface language and the entry kind (fresh installation, restored progress or later consent). A random event identifier only prevents duplicate delivery.

Below is the full list of fields Kifuku adds to individual events, under the names the server stores them with:

  • Active study day (active_day): sessions_bucket — a range for the number of completed sessions; level_stage — study stage (stage1…stage5, i.e. levels 1–5); used_ai — AI use (always “no” in Kifuku, which has no AI); saw_paywall — whether the full-version offer has been opened; premium — kind of unlocked material (none or full); ai_sub — AI subscription (always “no” in Kifuku); reminders_on — whether reminders are on and allowed by the system.
  • Session ended (session_ended): kind — session kind (new material, review, practice, single item); outcome — completed or abandoned; answers_bucket — a range for the number of answers; ai_checks — number of AI uses (always 0 in Kifuku); shown_by_type — ranges for the number of questions shown of two kinds: recognition (pattern, drop position, listening) and prediction from a rule. Only for an abandoned session: last_question_type — kind of the last question, last_answer — whether the last answer was correct, wrong or missing, wrong_streak_bucket — a range for the number of wrong answers in a row at the end.
  • First completed session (first_session_completed): minutes_bucket — a range for session length in minutes.
  • First consent (first_open): entry — entry kind.
  • Introduction closed (onboarding_done): action.
  • Offer viewed (paywall_viewed): trigger — where the offer was opened from (for example a locked lesson on the path, or Settings).
  • Purchase (purchase_completed): product_kind — product kind (full version).
  • Second session on another day (second_session_completed) and first review (first_review_completed) carry no extra fields.

Events contain no installation, user or device identifier, Japanese words, answers, notes, email address or payment details. Correctness reaches us only as the last answer and the wrong streak of an abandoned session, described above — without saying which questions they concerned. Events do not link activity across apps. The server does not store IP addresses in measurement data. Pending events remain on the device for up to 30 days, at most 500. The server retains events and deduplication keys for up to 400 days; aggregate statistics remain afterwards.

To limit abuse, the server uses a keyed hash of the IP address. Rate-limit entries are deleted after 24 hours; the hash is not included in events or aggregate statistics. Standard technical logs contain the request method, path, random request identifier and response status, without the IP address or learner answers.

Answer-card reports — from version 2.0

Reporting is optional. Opening the Report a problem sheet sends nothing. Only pressing Send transmits the report to https://api.jd-japanese.pl/v1/issue-reports. Before sending, you can preview the question, your answer and the displayed explanation, choose a reason and optionally add a comment. No account, purchase or usage-statistics consent is required. Sending, a failed attempt or cancelling does not change your grade, progress or review schedule.

A report contains the chosen reason, an optional comment (up to 2,000 characters) and a copy of one question: exercise type, question and word identifiers (with its group), content version, instructions, the word or recording file name from the question, available answers, your answer, the expected answer, the outcome and the displayed explanation. The explanation includes the “More” section with the group's rule. Your mnemonics are never sent. It also includes the app name, version, build number and interface language. A comment may contain whatever you type yourself — please do not include personal or confidential information.

We attach no person, installation, device or advertising identifier, no email address, and none of your answer history or study progress. Each report receives a random UUID so that retrying the same submission does not store it twice. The UUID identifies the report, not you; the server also records when it was received.

The content is stored in a separate reports register, available to authorised staff in a protected administration panel, and is used only to check and improve the material or grading. It is not sent to usage statistics. A daily cleanup removes the question copy, answer, comment and staff text notes 180 days after receipt. To keep unresolved problems in the queue, we retain a minimal record without that content: report UUID and app, dates, status, priority, backlog or linked-report references, planned and resolved versions, and a history of changed field names. This record remains until the case is closed and for 180 days after closure. Deleting the app or turning statistics off does not delete reports already sent.

The connection exposes your IP address to the server. Abuse protection limits submissions (20 per 10 minutes) using a secured digest of that address in a separate counter, without linking it to the report; rate-limit entries are deleted after 24 hours. Technical logs contain metadata such as route, status, processing time and request identifier, not report content.

Feedback form: “Suggest a feature” and “Report a bug” — from version 2.0

In Settings → Help you will find Suggest a feature and Report a bug. Both open a form inside the app. Opening the form sends nothing. Only pressing Send sends your message to https://api.jd-japanese.pl/v1/feedback. The form requires no account, no purchase and no usage-statistics consent.

A message contains its kind (idea or bug), a topic if you choose one, your text (up to 2,000 characters) and, for a bug, an optional description of what happened before it. Below the text field, under Sent with your message, you can see the remaining data before sending: the app name, its version and build number, and the interface language. The Include system and model switch is on by default and adds the iOS version and the phone model identifier (for example iPhone14,3) — a series name shared by many units, not a device identifier. You can turn it off. Each message receives a random UUID so retrying the same submission does not store it twice; the server also records when it was received. We attach no person, installation, device, advertising or purchase identifier, and no study history or progress. Your text may contain whatever you write yourself — please do not include confidential information.

Email address — only if you want a reply. You enter an address only after turning on I’d like a reply. Turning it on means you agree that we may reply to that address about this one message. We do not add the address to any mailing list or use it for anything else. Without the switch the app does not send an address, and the server rejects an address sent without that consent. After sending, the address does not stay in the app: unsent text waits on your device as a draft, the address does not.

The message is stored in a separate register, available to authorised staff in a protected administration panel. The email address is stored apart from the text, and the panel shows it only when a single message is opened, never in the list. The message is not sent to usage statistics, and its text and address are not written to server logs.

How long. We keep the address for 180 days together with the report, then delete it automatically. In the same daily cleanup, 180 days after receipt, we delete the message text, the description of what happened before the bug, the app version, build number, language, iOS version and phone model. To keep unresolved cases in the queue, a minimal record without that data remains: UUID and app, kind and topic, dates, status, priority, backlog or linked-message references, planned and resolved versions, and a history of changed field names. This record remains until the case is closed and for 180 days after closure. You can ask for earlier deletion by writing to kifuku@jd-japanese.pl. It is enough to give the email address you used in the form, or to describe the message so we can find it. If we reply to your address, that correspondence stays in our mailbox like any ordinary email; you can ask us to delete it too.

The connection exposes your IP address to the server. Abuse protection limits submissions (20 per 10 minutes) using a secured digest of that address in a separate counter, without linking it to the message; the IP address itself is not stored in the register. The technical logs described under answer-card reports apply to the form as well.

Messages prepared in your mail app

In two places Kifuku prepares an email in your device's mail app: Report the Japanese in Settings → Help, and the startup error screen (the message contains the error description). Nothing is sent until you press “Send” in your mail app yourself. Both messages end with the app version, build number, iOS version and device model identifier — the whole text is visible and you can delete those lines before sending. If you write this way, or directly to the app's address, I will see your email address and the message, as with any ordinary correspondence. Such messages are used only to reply.

Shared progress with other apps

If you have my other Japanese-learning apps on the same device, Kifuku writes one small progress summary file to an area shared between those apps, so it can show you how the whole family is going. The file holds numbers, not personal data, and never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes its entry.

Recordings and accent data

The app carries pronunciation recordings and accent data from open sources. They are embedded in the app file, so nothing is downloaded and nothing is streamed, and playing a recording sends no information anywhere. Authors and licences are credited inside the app, on the "Sources" screen.

Tracking and advertising

None. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and contains no advertising library or crash reporting. The only measurement is the optional usage statistics described above; an answer-card report and a feedback-form message are not measurement and are sent only after you press “Send”.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple. Kifuku never sees your payment details — it only asks StoreKit whether the purchase exists.

Notifications

They are optional and only remind you about reviews. The app works fully without them and does not ask for permission on first launch.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Usage statistics are optional and off by default. A voluntarily sent comment or feedback-form message can contain personal information only if you type it yourself; please don't. The email address in the feedback form is optional and is used only to reply.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. This page's address carries the app version, so older releases always point at the document that actually described them.
