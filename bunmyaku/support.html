Bunmyaku · last updated 23 August 2026

The quickest way to reach me is bunmyaku@jd-japanese.pl. Settings > Support has two buttons that open a pre-filled message.

Something in the Japanese looks wrong

Please tell me — this is the most useful kind of message I can get. A reading that teaches an untruth is the one mistake this app has no right to make, and it is the one a learner cannot spot on their own. Include the word and the sentence if you can.

A question felt unfair

If one of the wrong options looked like it could also be correct, that is a bug in the content, not in your Japanese. Some words genuinely have two readings and are meant to be excluded from reading questions; if one slipped through, I want to know.

I lost my progress

Progress lives on the device. If you still have the device, check Settings > Data > Import progress — if you exported a backup earlier, it restores everything including review dates. If you reinstalled without a backup, the progress is gone and I have no copy of it, because nothing is ever sent to a server.

I bought Premium and it is not unlocked

Settings > Premium > Restore purchases. It re-checks with the App Store on any device signed in with the same Apple Account. If that does not help, write to me with the date of purchase.

Will there be N2 and N1?

Yes, and the existing purchase covers them. There is no second payment.

The other apps

Kaname (grammar), Katsuyokei (conjugation), Joshi (particles) and Kazoekata (counters) are separate apps with separate purchases, by the same author.
