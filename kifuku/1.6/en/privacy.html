Privacy Policy

Kifuku 起伏 · version 1.6 · last updated: 1 November 2026

Kifuku 起伏 teaches Japanese pitch accent — where the tone drops inside a word. All learning happens on your device and works without an internet connection.

In short: no account and no advertising, and learning works offline. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and off by default. Your answers and progress stay on your device. Recordings are embedded in the app, not streamed.

Your account

There isn't one. Kifuku never asks you to register, sign in or give an email address, and has no way of recognising you.

Where your learning data goes

Your mastery of each item, your answer history and your review schedule stay on your device and never leave it. If you agree to usage statistics (below), only counts in ranges are sent — never the answers themselves.

Optional usage statistics

We ask once during first use or after an update. No events are collected or sent before consent. You can decline or withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy. Turning it off stops transmission and deletes pending events. Learning works the same after declining and without internet.

After consent, the app sends events to api.jd-japanese.pl about closing the introduction, sessions, reviews, active study days, viewing the Premium offer and a completed purchase. Events include the app version, interface language, learning level, first-use week, study-day number and approximate ranges for answer counts, question types and session duration. A random event identifier only prevents duplicate delivery.

Events contain no installation, user or device identifier, Japanese words, answers, notes or payment details. They do not link activity across apps. The server does not store IP addresses in measurement data. Pending events remain on the device for up to 30 days. The server retains events and deduplication keys for up to 400 days; aggregate statistics remain afterwards.

To limit abuse, the server uses a keyed hash of the IP address. Rate-limit entries are deleted after 24 hours; the hash is not included in events or aggregate statistics. Standard technical logs contain the request method, path, random request identifier and response status, without the IP address or learner answers.

Shared progress with other apps

If you have my other Japanese-learning apps on the same device, Kifuku writes one small progress summary file to an area shared between those apps, so it can show you how the whole family is going. The file holds numbers, not personal data, and never leaves the device. Deleting the app deletes its entry.

Recordings and accent data

The app carries pronunciation recordings and accent data from open sources. They are embedded in the app file, so nothing is downloaded and nothing is streamed, and playing a recording sends no information anywhere. Authors and licences are credited inside the app, on the "Sources" screen.

Tracking and advertising

None. The app does not track you across other apps or websites, does not use the advertising identifier, and contains no advertising library or crash reporting. The only measurement is the optional usage statistics described above.

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled entirely by Apple. Kifuku never sees your payment details — it only asks StoreKit whether the purchase exists.

Notifications

They are optional and only remind you about reviews. The app works fully without them and does not ask for permission on first launch.

Children

The app is rated 4+. Usage statistics are optional and off by default.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top changes with it. This page's address carries the app version, so older releases always point at the document that actually described them.
