Joshi drills Japanese particles: は, が, を, に, で and the rest. Below are answers to the questions that come up most often. If yours isn't here — write.

The quickest way to reach me is joshi@jd-japanese.pl. In Settings → Help there are Suggest a feature and Report a problem forms — nothing goes until you tap Send, and you give an email address only if you want a reply.

The app says my answer is wrong, and I'm sure it's right

Report it — it's the most important report there is. Below the answer card there is a Report a problem link: choose the reason My answer should be accepted and tap Send. The report carries the sentence, your answer and the explanation shown, so you don't need to copy anything out. Particles can be interchangeable, and a sentence in which two of them are correct should never have made it into practice. Reports like that take priority over everything else.

Why isn't the variant I expected offered for some sentences?

Because the wrong variants have to be genuinely wrong in that sentence. A particle that gives a correct sentence with a different meaning is not a wrong variant — it is a second answer. That is why you won't see も offered in sentences with は, even though both can fit there.

How does it know I confuse に with で, rather than just knowing に badly?

Because the app tracks three things separately: the particle itself, its specific role, and the pair. A gap sentence whose variants are に and で is evidence about that pair — and only sentences like that move it. That is why the statistics can show に ↔ で as your weakest pair instead of separate percentages for に and for で.

Why do I see the translation only after I answer?

Because with the translation in view you match the particle to your own language instead of reading the Japanese — and practice something other than you think. The translation shows up after your answer, never while you are working one out. In Settings, in the Translation row, you can choose whether, after your answer, it shows: Always, On tap or Never.

Why don't the sections match the JLPT?

Because particles don't divide up that way. に for a time is N5, for a purpose N4, in the passive higher still — and you learn them side by side, because they get confused with each other, not with material from their own level. The six sections on the map describe how the material actually builds up: 40 roles of 12 particles and 14 confusable pairs.

What is free, and what does the purchase open?

Sections 1–3 are entirely free: 20 of the 40 roles, 535 of the 998 sentences and the first checkpoint. The one-time full-version purchase opens sections 4–6 and the second checkpoint, with no renewals. A full version bought in Joshi 1.x (formerly “Premium”) stays yours and opens sections 4–6 now too.

I lost my streak even though I practiced

The study day starts at the hour set in Settings → Advanced → A new day starts at (4:00 by default), not at midnight — a session at 0:30 still belongs to the evening before. If you're going away, turn on Holiday mode in the Study plan: your streak and weekly goal wait on hold.

What does AI help in “My sentences” do?

“My sentences” is a notebook: sentences saved from a question card, typed in yourself or written after a round. It works offline. Optional AI help takes a sentence from the notebook — from a manga, subtitles, a sign or your own head — and shows what its particles are doing. “Explain with AI” takes the sentence as correct and gives each particle a role, named after a role from this app. “Check with AI” assesses your own sentence: each particle is right, wrong, or “works, but…”, with a short why and the particle to use. Only the particles are checked. A sentence can be at most 120 characters. The answer is written by a language model and does not count towards your progress.

An AI help result looks wrong

The explanations and assessments are written by a language model, and it can get them wrong — in both directions. Write in, and paste your sentence and which mode was used (“Explain with AI” or “Check with AI”): the server does not keep your sentence, so without that I have no way to reproduce what happened. The sentence and the result are waiting in “My sentences”. The result does not count towards your progress, so a model mistake breaks nothing in your learning.

I don't see the AI help buttons

AI help appears only on a device that confirms the app is genuine through Apple's App Attest. On a device that does not support it there are no buttons, and “My sentences” and the rest of the app work unchanged. AI help itself needs the internet.

Scanning a photo doesn't find the text

Text in a photo is recognized on the phone, so a close, straight, well-lit shot helps. Recognition can be imperfect — read the sentence before sending it; you can fix it in the field. You can always type it in yourself.

Free requests and the subscription

Everyone gets five free requests a month, with or without the full version. When they run out, the app shows when they come back. An answer with nothing to show — when the sentence has no particle — does not use up a request. More requests come with the AI help subscription, monthly or yearly. It renews automatically until you turn it off — in your Apple Account settings or with the Manage subscription button, at least a day before the paid period ends. The subscription does not open sections, and the full version does not include the subscription.

I bought it and the app doesn't see it

Use Restore purchases — it's in Settings, on the Purchases card, and on the subscription screen. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so they come back on any device signed in with the same account. If that doesn't help, write to me with the purchase date. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

You don't have to. Progress is stored on the device only, but Settings → Your data → Progress backup has “Export progress” and “Import progress” — move the file any way you like. Importing replaces the progress on the new device and adds the sentences from “My sentences” to the ones already there. Backups from Joshi 1.x open too. Your purchase travels with your Apple Account. There is no iCloud sync. “Your examples” on role pages are not part of the backup.

Does the app work offline?

All of the learning and the “My sentences” notebook — yes. AI help, purchases and sending a report from the answer card or a message from the form need the internet. Voluntary usage statistics send data only after consent; offline they wait locally for up to 30 days, at most 500 events. Being offline changes nothing in your learning or progress.

Privacy

No account and no ads. AI help uses a server that does not store the text of your sentences. Anonymous usage statistics are separate and off by default. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Your data, using Send anonymous usage statistics. Turning it off stops collection and delivery and deletes the unsent queue and the current session record used for measurement. A report from the answer card and a message from the form leave only when you tap Send; their content is deleted after 180 days. Your full progress and answer history stay on the device.
