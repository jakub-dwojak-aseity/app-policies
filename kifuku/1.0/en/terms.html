What this app is

Kifuku 起伏 is a tool for learning Japanese pitch accent. It teaches the Tokyo standard — the accent of dictionaries, broadcasting and textbooks. It is one standard of Japanese, not the only one: in Kansai many of these words carry a different accent, and that is not a mistake in either direction. The app says so on its own screens.

What the app does not promise

Accent can have more than one accepted value, and descriptions differ between dictionaries. The app shows, for every rule, how often it holds — precisely so that it does not promise a law without exceptions. It is not language advice and not preparation for any examination.
