Support

Kifuku 起伏 · version 2.0 · last updated: 15 November 2026

I write everything myself, so you get a reply from me — usually within a few days.

Fastest route: kifuku@jd-japanese.pl. From version 2.0, the app has Suggest a feature and Report a bug forms in Settings → Help — your message comes straight to me, without a mail app.

Reporting straight from the app — from version 2.0

Choosing a topic is optional. Below the text you can see what is sent with your message (app version, language and, if you leave the switch on, iOS version and phone model). Nothing is sent until you press Send. If you want a reply, turn on I’d like a reply and enter your email address — I will write to it only about that message. Without an address I cannot reply, and changes show up in “What’s new” after an update. Details, 180-day retention and deletion on request: privacy policy.

I found a mistake in the content

This is the report I care about most. The easiest way is from the answer card: Report a problem shows what will be sent (the question, your answer, the explanation), and only Send sends it; your grade and progress do not change. You can also use Report the Japanese in Settings → Help, or write to me. Tell me which word, what accent the app showed, and what you believe is correct. The accent data comes from UniDic and can differ from other dictionaries — if you have a source at hand, name it.

There is no recording for an entry

For some entries the button says "coming later". That is not a failure: we have as many recordings as an open source could give, and the app deliberately uses no speech synthesis — the system does not guarantee it will read 箸 and 橋 differently, and that difference is the whole point.

I lost my purchase

The purchase screen has "Restore purchases". The purchase belongs to your Apple ID, so it comes back on any device you are signed in on.

I changed devices

Progress lives on the device, not on a server. Settings has a progress backup — make one on the old device and load it on the new one.

The app is not working

Tell me what happens and on which screen. The easiest way is the Report a bug form in Settings → Help — it adds the app version for you. The version and build are also at the very bottom of the Settings screen.
