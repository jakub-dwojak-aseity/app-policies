Estimates, not guarantees

SpoolCalc works out how much line will fit on a reel from the relationship capacity ∝ length × diameter². That relationship is a good approximation, but a real spool is not a perfect model. Results vary because of:

  • braid flattening and compressing under tension;
  • real line diameter differing from the figure printed on the box;
  • manufacturing tolerances between and within brands;
  • tapered, long-cast and otherwise non-cylindrical spool geometry;
  • how tightly the line is wound.

Treat every result as a starting point. Leave room below the spool lip, and verify as you wind. The app is a calculation aid and nothing more; it is not tackle advice and does not replace the manufacturer's specification for your reel.
