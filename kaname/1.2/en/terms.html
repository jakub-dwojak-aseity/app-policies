What this app is

Kaname is a tool for studying Japanese grammar on your own, at JLPT levels N5 through N1. The learning — reviews, every question type, the study plan and your progress — runs entirely on your device and without an internet connection. Version 1.2 adds one optional feature, AI analysis, which uses a server and which you start yourself. This is not a taught course, a certification service, or preparation that guarantees passing the JLPT.

AI analysis — an auto-renewing subscription

AI analysis is a separate, optional feature: it checks a sentence you write yourself and identifies grammar patterns in text. It is the only part of the app that costs something every time it is used, which is why it is billed as a subscription rather than sold outright.

Your data

Your progress and the sentences you add are saved only on your device. Keeping them safe is up to you — deleting the app deletes that data. Use Export data in Settings to keep a copy.

AI analysis is the exception, and only when you start it: the pattern being practiced and the sentence you wrote are sent to the server. Your study history is not, nothing that could identify you is, and a photo never is — text in a photo is read by the phone itself. What is stored, and for how long, is described in the privacy policy.

Accuracy of the content

The grammar material was prepared with due care, but natural language is more complex than any simplification of it. Explanations and examples are educational and do not constitute a linguistic norm. If you find an error, write in — corrections go into later releases.

This applies to AI analysis separately: its answers are generated by a language model and can be wrong. They are a hint, not an exam grade or a teacher's opinion — treat them as a starting point for checking, not as a verdict.
